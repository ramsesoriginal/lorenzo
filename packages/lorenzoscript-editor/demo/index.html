✧ LorenzoScript

The editor from packages/lorenzoscript-editor, over an in-memory stand-in for Lorenzo: it knows ashfang and emberdeep, and one date in the Harptos calendar. Everything else resolves to nothing, as it would for a reader who may not see it.
