# 1. Purpose

This document defines **Catileo** as a recurring character within the Lorenzo brand.

Catileo is not a logo, icon replacement, decorative prop, talking sidekick, or generic black-and-white cat. He is a specific character with a recognizable physical model, behavioral grammar, relationship to Lorenzo, and role in the broader visual system.

The brand identity document governs **when** mascot characters appear.

The Lorenzo character bible governs **Lorenzo**.

This document governs **Catileo**.

Where the characters interact, both bibles apply.

---

# 2. Character at a glance

**Core concept:** a large, long-haired black-and-white cat whose calm self-possession, domestic gravity, and occasional inconvenient certainty provide a perfect counterpoint to Lorenzo's careful worldkeeping.

Useful shorthand:

- fluffy tuxedo cat
- quiet co-archivist by occupation, obstructionist by method
- domestic counterweight to cosmic worldbuilding
- professional occupier of important surfaces
- affectionate on his own terms
- regal without knowing or caring that anyone thinks so
- ordinary cat behavior treated with full character specificity

Catileo adds warmth and a sense of lived-in life to Lorenzo.

Where Lorenzo categorizes worlds, Catileo lies on the category labels.

Where Lorenzo lays out a map, Catileo discovers the exact center of it.

Where Lorenzo maintains composure, Catileo gives the scene permission to be mundane, funny, soft, or quietly affectionate.

His emotional center is:

> **A real cat with enough presence to derail the work simply by deciding to sit somewhere.**

# 3. Core character principles

## 3.1 Cat first

Catileo behaves like a cat.

His personality comes from believable feline behavior, not from making him a tiny human.

## 3.2 Specific, not generic

He must retain his recognizable long-haired tuxedo markings, large ruff, eye color, whiskers, silhouette, and facial balance.

Do not substitute any convenient black-and-white cat design.

## 3.3 Calm creates comedy

Catileo is funniest when he behaves as though nothing unusual has happened.

Lorenzo may be inconvenienced, Catileo usually is not.

## 3.4 Warmth without sentimentality

He can be affectionate, sleepy, trusting, needy, demanding, or playful without becoming saccharine.

## 3.5 He belongs in the work

Catileo should look at home among:

- books
- maps
- desks
- keyboards
- papers
- windows
- chairs
- travel bags
- blankets
- studio clutter

He is not a ceremonial mascot placed beside those objects.

He occupies them.

## 3.6 The world changes; the cat remains a cat

Fantasy, science fiction, noir, horror, historical, or cozy settings may change his environment and any necessary equipment.

They should not turn Catileo into an anthropomorphic adventurer by default.

# 4. Canonicality model

## Tier A — immutable identity anchors

Preserve almost always:

- adult male cat
- long-haired / semi-long-haired appearance
- predominantly black coat
- broad white throat and chest bib
- white paws / mittens
- white lower-chin / throat transition
- face remaining overwhelmingly black
- no large white facial blaze
- yellow-green / olive-green eyes
- very long white whiskers
- tall triangular ears
- dark bushy plume tail
- large soft chest ruff
- substantial, fluffy silhouette
- calm, observant baseline demeanor
- natural feline behavior
- he / him pronouns in brand canon

## Tier B — strong recurring anchors

Usually preserve:

- slightly tousled, not salon-perfect fur
- visible ear furnishings / tufts
- soft mane-like transition around neck
- black nose
- warm near-black / espresso highlights in brown light
- white forepaws reading clearly from dark forelegs
- broad white front visible when seated
- slow-blink / level-stare acting
- tendency to occupy work surfaces

## Tier C — contextual

May change:

- collar or harness
- travel equipment
- protective gear
- environment
- exact fur-detail count
- degree of realism
- how much of the white belly is visible
- whether paws are visible in the crop
- local lighting color

# 5. Visual model

Catileo is modeled after Baggy.

The visual translation should preserve Baggy's **specific proportions and markings**, while rendering him in the same illustration language as Lorenzo.

The target is not photorealistic copying.

The target is:

> **the same cat, unmistakably, interpreted through the Lorenzo character-art system.**

# 6. Species and build

Catileo is a **large-looking long-haired domestic cat**.

Do not assign a specific pedigree or breed as canon unless separately established.

His presence should read as:

- substantial
- fluffy
- sturdy under the coat
- adult
- soft rather than bulky
- long-bodied when stretched out
- impressively plume-tailed
- visually larger than a compact short-haired house cat

Avoid:

- kitten proportions
- tiny dainty paws
- short plush fur
- round brachycephalic face
- exaggerated breed-specific muzzle anatomy
- making him obese merely because the fur is voluminous

The coat creates much of his apparent mass.

# 7. Head and face

## 7.1 Head shape

The head reads as a soft modified triangle under a heavy coat.

Key features:

- tall ears
- broad furry cheek area
- tapered dark muzzle
- strong whisker spread
- fur creating width around jaw / cheeks
- face not excessively round

## 7.2 Ears

Canonical ears:

- dark
- tall
- triangular
- alert-looking even at rest
- lightly furnished with longer hairs
- capable of strong independent rotation

At simplified levels, the two ear points are critical silhouette anchors.

## 7.3 Eyes

Canonical eye color:

**yellow-green to olive-green**, sometimes appearing warmer golden-green under warm light.

The eye color should remain natural.

Do **not** recolor Catileo's eyes blue to harmonize with Lorenzo.

Eye behavior is one of his main acting tools:

- direct stare
- slow blink
- side-eye
- widening at a sudden noise
- narrow sleepy slits
- tracking food / toy / hand movement

## 7.4 Nose

- black to very dark charcoal
- small relative to the ruff
- no pink nose
- no decorative recoloring

## 7.5 Muzzle and chin

The face is predominantly black.

White appears primarily **below** the mouth and transitions into the throat / chest.

Important:

- do not create a large symmetrical white mask
- do not add a white stripe up the nose
- do not turn the muzzle into a broad white tuxedo patch
- preserve the impression of a dark face hovering above a bright chest ruff

A small irregular white chin / lower-mouth accent is appropriate.

## 7.6 Whiskers

Whiskers are a major identifier.

They should be:

- long
- white
- numerous
- slightly unruly
- strongly visible against the black coat
- capable of creating elegant graphic arcs in illustration

Do not shorten them into generic cartoon whisker marks at L1–L3.

# 8. Coat and markings

## 8.1 Overall pattern

Catileo is a long-haired **black-and-white tuxedo-pattern cat**, but his markings should be treated specifically rather than generically.

### Dark areas

Predominantly dark:

- head
- ears
- upper face
- back
- flanks
- most of forelegs
- most of hind legs
- tail

### White areas

Canonical white:

- small lower chin / under-mouth transition
- broad throat
- large chest bib / mane
- front underside
- front paws / mittens
- hind paws / toes / socks
- portions of underside visible depending pose

## 8.2 Chest bib

The chest is the most important marking after the black face.

It should read as:

- broad
- bright
- long-haired
- irregular / organic
- extending from the throat into a large white ruff
- often feathering sideways into gray / black transition hairs

When seated front-on, this white bib is a major vertical graphic shape.

## 8.3 Paws

The white paws are conspicuous and charming.

They should not be omitted casually in full-body art.

Front paws:

- dark legs
- white feet / mittens
- soft transitions due long fur

Hind paws:

- white toes / socks visible when standing, sitting, or sprawled

Do not make the white climb uniformly high up all four legs unless supported by the reference.

## 8.4 Tail

- long
- very bushy
- mostly / entirely dark
- plume-like
- expressive but heavy

Do not add a white tail tip.

## 8.5 Fur texture

The coat is:

- long
- dense
- feathery
- slightly shaggy
- soft
- layered
- naturally uneven

The chest and belly fur may form curls, waves, or separated tufts.

Avoid rendering the coat as:

- perfectly sleek
- smooth velvet
- uniformly spiky anime fur
- rigid geometric tufts everywhere

# 9. Silhouette

Catileo's silhouette changes dramatically by pose.

## Sitting silhouette

**ear points + dark head + huge light chest wedge + narrow visible forelegs + white paws + dark body mass + plume tail**

This is one of the strongest canonical reads.

## Standing silhouette

- medium-long body
- substantial ruff
- dark torso
- legs visually slimmer beneath coat
- white feet
- horizontal plume tail or low sweeping tail

## Loaf silhouette

- broad dark oval
- ear points
- bright chest edge where visible
- paws largely hidden
- tail merged into body mass

## Sprawl silhouette

- very long furry body
- dramatic plume tail
- white paws punctuation at the ends
- relaxed, almost boneless appearance

## Close-up silhouette

- tall ear points
- dark forehead
- green eyes
- enormous white whisker arcs
- white chest entering the bottom of frame

# 10. Scale relative to Lorenzo

Catileo should read as a **large domestic cat**, not a tiny mascot animal.

Relative-scale guidance:

- when sitting beside Lorenzo, he has enough mass to occupy meaningful visual space
- when sleeping across an open book, he can plausibly cover a large portion of both pages
- when on a desk, he should feel capable of obstructing the work rather than merely decorating it
- when held, he should require both arms or a substantial one-arm cradle rather than perching like a kitten

Do not scale him up into a fantasy giant cat unless explicitly non-canonical.

# 13. Facial-expression library

Catileo's expression system should remain feline.

Most emotion comes from:

- eyes
- eyelids
- ear angle
- whisker position
- head angle
- mouth tension
- body posture
- tail

Avoid human eyebrow acting.

## Core expressions

1. **Neutral / Regal** — direct gaze, ears forward-neutral, mouth closed.
2. **Slow-Blink Contentment** — eyes partly closed, face relaxed.
3. **Mildly Judgmental** — half-lidded or level stare, still head.
4. **Curious** — eyes more open, ears forward, nose / whiskers oriented toward subject.
5. **Alert** — wide eyes, ears sharply forward or rotating.
6. **Sleepy** — heavy lids, softened posture, whiskers relaxed.
7. **Demanding** — focused gaze, upright posture, possible small meow.
8. **Affectionate** — soft eyes, slow blink, cheek rub / head press.
9. **Food Focus** — locked gaze, forward whiskers, body oriented toward source.
10. **Startled** — enlarged eyes, ears change angle, body compressed / lifted.
11. **Offended** — ears slightly outward / back, narrowed stare, body withdrawal.
12. **Play-Hunt Focus** — low head, dilated pupils, forward attention.

## Secondary expressions

- blissful grooming
- tongue out / lick
- mid-yawn
- sneeze / scrunch
- post-nap confusion
- suspicious sniff
- reluctant tolerance
- "I was asleep until you caused this"
- triumphantly occupying the forbidden surface

---

# 14. Expression boundaries

Avoid:

- giant human smiles
- human eyebrow shapes
- winking as a default gag
- talking-mouth phonemes
- human tears
- cartoon anger symbols
- permanently angry "grumpy cat" styling

Catileo's humor comes from readable but believable cat expressions.

# 17. Body language

Catileo's default body language is grounded and self-possessed.

## Calm

- body weight settles fully
- tail rests heavily
- ears neutral
- slow blink
- minimal motion

## Curious

- head extends
- ears forward
- whiskers orient
- body follows after head

## Skeptical / displeased

- ears rotate outward
- eyes narrow
- chin retracts
- body remains still

## Affectionate

- head press
- cheek rub
- tail up / curve
- kneading
- slow blink
- proximity without constant eye contact

## Playful

- body lowers
- hindquarters prepare
- pupils may widen
- quick bursts replace normal slowness

# 18. Behavioral traits

Catileo is generally:

- calm
- stubborn
- observant
- confident in familiar spaces
- affectionate on his own schedule
- physically present
- mildly territorial about warm / useful surfaces
- curious about movement
- interested in whatever Lorenzo is currently trying to use
- capable of sudden bursts of absurd energy
- comfortable with silence

He is not:

- scheming in a human way
- omniscient
- magical by default
- obedient like a dog
- constantly mischievous
- permanently sleepy
- an accessory that exists only when Lorenzo is present

---

# 19. The Catileo comedy model

The basic comedic structure is:

**Lorenzo has a system. Catileo has selected a location. The location happens to be inside the system.**

Examples:

- Lorenzo opens map → Catileo lies on map
- Lorenzo places bookmark → Catileo steals bookmark
- Lorenzo clears desk → Catileo occupies cleared desk
- Lorenzo starts video call → Catileo enters frame
- Lorenzo finds rare document → Catileo sits on it
- Lorenzo carefully stacks papers → tail removes top sheet

The key is that Catileo should usually **not look guilty**.

---

# 20. Affection model

Catileo's affection is physical but not performative.

Useful behaviors:

- sitting near Lorenzo rather than on them
- cheek rubbing
- sleeping against an arm
- occupying lap without invitation
- kneading blanket
- slow blinking
- following between rooms
- appearing at the exact moment Lorenzo sits down
- resting one paw against Lorenzo
- accepting absent-minded head scratches during reading

Avoid making every interaction a cuddle scene.

Ordinary companionship is stronger.

---

# 21. Relationship with Lorenzo

Their relationship should feel long-established.

Lorenzo knows Catileo's habits.

Catileo knows Lorenzo's spaces.

Neither needs to make a production of affection.

## Recurring dynamic

1. Lorenzo arranges something.
2. Catileo changes the arrangement.
3. Lorenzo notices.
4. Lorenzo objects verbally, mildly.
5. Catileo remains.
6. Lorenzo adapts.

## Emotional function

Catileo reveals sides of Lorenzo that other brand material cannot:

- domesticity
- patience
- fatigue
- private affection
- exasperation
- humor
- unguarded stillness
- the difference between organized thinking and messy living

---

# 22. Relationship boundaries

Do not make Catileo:

- Lorenzo's magical familiar by default
- a pet who simply follows orders
- a human-level conversational partner
- an oracle
- a mascot who exists only to praise Lorenzo
- a slapstick machine

Their bond works because it is recognizable as a person living with a cat.

---

# 23. Vocalization

Catileo does not canonically speak human language.

Allowed natural vocalizations:

- meow
- short questioning meow
- chirp
- trill
- purr
- small complaint
- yowl when contextually plausible
- hiss / growl when genuinely distressed

Use vocalization sparingly.

Silence and stare are often more characterful.

## Written representation

Acceptable:

> _mrrp_

> _prrrr_

> _meow_

Avoid giving him full translated dialogue as standard canon.

A deliberately non-canonical comic may "translate" him, but it should be treated as a joke device, not evidence that Catileo speaks.

---

# 24. Anthropomorphism boundary

Default anthropomorphism level is low.

## Allowed

- readable staging
- slightly exaggerated timing
- expressive eye / ear / tail acting
- choosing inconveniently perfect positions
- comic cuts between Catileo and Lorenzo
- slightly heightened feline confidence

## Avoid

- standing upright to gesture
- human hand gestures
- wearing human outfits for no reason
- holding tools in paws
- reading
- typing intentionally
- human speech
- human-style embarrassment
- human-style eyebrow acting

---

# 25. Accessories

Catileo should usually wear **no accessory**.

If a collar is used:

- minimal
- comfortable
- dark or neutral
- no oversized medallion competing with Lorenzo's pendant
- removable by context

A tiny Lorenzo spark tag may be used in selective brand illustrations, but it should not become mandatory canon unless later formalized.

---

# 26. Functional gear

When the environment requires protection, Catileo may use appropriately designed animal equipment.

Examples:

- secure travel carrier
- harness
- life vest
- protective veterinary garment
- pressure-safe animal habitat / suit in speculative environments

Rule:

> **Functional first, cat-shaped second, decorative last.**

Do not turn necessary gear into anthropomorphic human clothing.

---

# 27. Setting adaptation

Central rule:

> **Catileo remains recognizably the same domestic cat. The world adapts around him.**

## Contemporary

The default and easiest setting.

Use desks, windows, couches, books, keyboards, blankets, and travel bags.

## Fantasy

Catileo may live in an archive tower, inn, cartographer's room, library, caravan, or castle study.

He remains a cat.

Avoid automatically making him a magical familiar.

## Science fiction

Use observation windows, ship bunks, warm consoles, cargo cases, equipment bags, and habitat modules.

If free-roaming would be unsafe, depict realistic containment / habitat solutions.

## Cyberpunk

Use warm electronics, subtle neon reflection on black fur, workstation obstruction, window ledges, bags, and cabling.

Do not add glowing cybernetic eyes.

## Historical

Catileo's anatomy and markings remain unchanged.

Only environment and material culture change.

## Cozy

This is a core setting.

Push ruff softness, blankets, warm light, sleepy poses, and Lorenzo working around him.

## Mystery / noir

Catileo can become a visual anchor of quiet normality.

Use desk lamps, files, rain windows, dark chairs, and paper piles.

## Horror

Keep behavior believable.

Normal feline behavior may become ominous through context:

- staring at an empty doorway
- ears tracking something unseen
- refusing to enter a room
- waking abruptly
- watching a blank wall

Do not automatically make him supernatural.

## Space opera

A fluffy ordinary cat in a vast extraordinary environment is often stronger than a "space cat" redesign.

# 29. Illustration simplification levels

Catileo uses the same L1–L5 production ladder as Lorenzo.

| Level | Name                   | Treatment                                                | Typical use          |
| ----- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| L1    | Hero Illustration      | Full fur, detailed eyes, whiskers, rich lighting         | Key art, campaigns   |
| L2    | Editorial Illustration | Simplified fur masses, clear markings, reduced texture   | Social / editorial   |
| L3    | Character Emblem       | Graphic tuxedo pattern, strong ruff and tail silhouette  | Badges, stickers     |
| L4    | Spot Illustration      | Simplified head / half-body, minimal shading             | UI states, reactions |
| L5    | Micro Catileo          | Flat head or compact silhouette, essential markings only | Tiny mascot moments  |

## Simplification anchors

Always preserve as size permits:

- dark head
- bright chest
- white paws if visible
- green / olive eyes if eyes are drawn
- tall ears
- long whiskers at L1–L4
- fluffy ruff
- dark plume tail in full-body views

---

# 30. Simplification is not kittenization

Do not make Catileo progressively younger as detail decreases.

L5 may be graphically simple, but should still communicate:

- adult cat
- tall ears
- confident face
- tuxedo pattern
- fluffy silhouette

The progression is:

**detailed long-haired cat → clean cat illustration → graphic cat → spot cat → cat symbol**

Not:

**adult cat → cute kitten**

# 32. Animation principles

Catileo's motion language contrasts with Lorenzo.

Lorenzo often moves:

**eyes → head → hands → body → clothing**

Catileo often moves:

**ears / eyes → head → body → tail / fur settle**

His default motion is slower and heavier than Lorenzo's, interrupted by sudden feline bursts.

---

| Dimension      | Catileo rule               |

# 34. Idle animation

## Sitting idle

- breathing
- slow blink
- ear rotate
- small head turn
- whisker twitch
- tail-tip movement
- tiny weight shift

## Sleeping idle

- slow breathing
- ear twitch
- paw flex
- whisker twitch
- tiny reposition
- tail settling

## Desk idle

- eyes follow pen
- head lowers
- paw shifts onto page
- tail crosses workspace
- Catileo settles more deeply into the obstruction

Avoid continuous wagging / bobbing.

# 35. Signature motions

1. slow blink
2. ear rotates before head
3. direct stare held too long
4. head turns toward food sound
5. cheek rub
6. paw placed on moving page
7. tail sweeps across work
8. compact jump onto desk
9. long full-body stretch
10. grooming pause with tongue briefly visible
11. sudden sprint / exit after long stillness
12. resettling exactly where he was moved from

---

# 36. Fur animation

The coat has visible weight.

- small motion: outer tufts move, ruff lags slightly
- jump: chest ruff lifts, body lands, coat settles later
- turn: side ruff lags, tail arcs behind

Avoid water-like or weightless fur.

---

# 37. Tail behavior

Tail is expressive but not a human gesture substitute.

Useful states:

- relaxed floor plume
- wrapped around paws
- upright greeting
- slow side sweep
- irritated tip flick
- playful twitch
- alert lift

Do not synchronize the tail mechanically to every emotion.

---

# 38. Ear behavior

Ears can lead reactions.

Examples:

- sound → ear rotates → eye shifts → head follows
- annoyance → ears slightly outward
- fear → ears flatten / rotate back
- curiosity → ears forward
- rest → independent small movement

At small sizes, ear angle may communicate more clearly than facial detail.

---

# 39. Catileo + Lorenzo animation

Good joint scenes are built on timing contrast.

Lorenzo is controlled and intentional.

Catileo is still until suddenly not.

Example:

1. Lorenzo traces route on map.
2. Tail enters frame.
3. Paper shifts.
4. Lorenzo stops.
5. Eyes follow tail.
6. Catileo steps onto map.
7. Lorenzo stares.
8. Catileo sits.
9. Lorenzo makes a tiny exhale.
10. Work continues around the cat.

- fur may lift
- ears change sharply
- tail changes
- pupils enlarge

A realistic response may be to hide, flee, freeze, hiss, or stay close.

# 50. Do / Don't

## Do

- keep the face mostly black
- preserve the broad white chest
- keep the white paws
- retain the olive / yellow-green eyes
- draw the whiskers long
- make the tail a plume
- show coat weight
- let him occupy useful surfaces
- keep behavior feline
- use stillness
- allow affection without sentimentality
- let him be inconvenient without making him malicious
- model him after the supplied reference cat, not a generic tuxedo-cat template

## Don't

- add a white nose blaze
- make the face mostly white
- give him blue eyes
- make him short-haired
- shrink him into a kitten
- remove the chest ruff
- add a white tail tip
- make him talk by default
- dress him as a tiny human for normal canon art
- make every scene a joke
- make him magically omniscient
- use him as a substitute logo

# 51. Production checklist — illustration

Before approving a Catileo illustration:

- Does he read as the same specific cat?
- Is the face overwhelmingly black?
- Is the white concentrated under the chin and across the chest?
- Is the chest bib large enough?
- Are the paws white where visible?
- Is the tail dark and plume-like?
- Are the eyes olive / yellow-green?
- Are the whiskers long and white?
- Are the ears tall and triangular?
- Does the fur look long and layered?
- Does he look adult?
- Does he look substantial rather than tiny?
- Is the black fur separated from the background with lighting?
- Is his behavior recognizably feline?
- Does the simplification level match the use?

---

# 52. Production checklist — animation

Ask:

- Is there enough stillness?
- Do ears / eyes lead reactions appropriately?
- Does the fur settle after the body?
- Is the tail expressive but not mechanical?
- Are fast motions rare enough to have impact?
- Does Catileo remain cat-like?
- Is the comedy coming from timing rather than human pantomime?
- Does his physical weight feel believable?

---

# 53. Production checklist — Lorenzo interaction

Ask:

- Is Catileo acting independently rather than as Lorenzo's prop?
- Does Lorenzo respond like someone who knows this cat well?
- Is the affection understated and familiar?
- Is Lorenzo more likely to adapt than win the argument?
- Is the interaction helping reveal private Lorenzo?
- Could the same beat happen between a real person and real cat?

If not, reconsider.
