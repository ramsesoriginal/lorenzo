# 0428 - The account hub moves into the web shell

Status: accepted, decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-06. Part of [RFC 0050](../rfcs/0050-the-web-interface.md); it implements UI-D ([#1015](https://github.com/ramsesoriginal/lorenzo/issues/1015)) with [ADR 0416](0416-first-run-the-account-menu-and-joining.md), [0417](0417-account-hub-redesign.md) and [0418](0418-profile-access-tokens-and-connecting-a-device.md), on the shell of [ADR 0426](0426-the-web-shell-grows-in-app-shell-opt-in.md).

## Context

The account hub is the one app that does not use `@lorenzo/app-shell`: it has its own head, header, footer and navigation, with links that appear only when the person may have them. The account menu of ADR 0416 points at the hub's pages (Profile, Notifications, Libraries and people, Access tokens and devices), so the hub is the first app to move over.

## Decision

- **The hub's frame is the web shell** (`AppPage shell="web"`). Its own header, footer and head are removed; its pages keep their content and move to the new designs one slice at a time.
- **Gated links are the shell's.** A `NavItem` may say `requires: "signed-in"` (or `library-admin`, `repository-work`, `discover`, `create-tenant`, `beings`); the link stays hidden until the app calls `shell.allow(requires)`. The hub keeps the rules that decide which pages a person has (`GET /me`, `GET /tenants`, the discover facets) in `lib/shell.ts`, unchanged. An answer that does not come leaves its links hidden.
- **`welcome` is optional on `AppPage`.** The hub's pages say their own loading, so the frame shows the page at once; the other apps keep their welcome.
- **The bell is the hub's unread count** (`shell.notifications`), asked again every 30 seconds, when the tab returns and when a notification is marked read, as before.
- **The slices** after the frame: Profile (ADR 0418, PT2, PT3, PT6), Access tokens and Connect a device (PT4, PT5), Home with first run and "caught up" (AH1), then Libraries, campaigns and notifications, and Repositories as one area (AH2 to AH9) once each is agreed.

## Consequences

- The hub depends on `@lorenzo/app-shell`; the web shell's account menu links to the hub's current paths until the pages move.
- The frame changes what every hub page looks like around its content. The end-to-end checks that name the header (`banner`, the navigation's name "Pages") follow it.
- **Profile (the first page redesigned):** a caption on every group of fields says who sees it ("Others see this" with an eye, "Only you and admins see this" with a lock); a card beside the form shows how others see the person (picture, name, colour and a notification line, never pronouns, bio or languages, which others do not see) and moves above the form below 1100px. Each field saves as it is changed and says "Saved." beside it, with an Undo that puts back what it held; a refusal is shown beside the field and the draft stays. The colour is five swatches and Custom…, and a custom one that cannot be told from a light or a dark page (3 to 1) is turned away in words. The tokens and delete sections stay on the page until their own slice.
- **Access tokens (its own page):** `/tokens/` lists the tokens with the name, the last four characters and a state in words and an icon ("Last used today", "Not used for 120 days", "Never used", "Expires in 4 days", "Expired 3 weeks ago"); a token not used for 90 days is tinted and one that has ended is struck through. One sentence says a token is a whole key. Revoke asks in a dialog that names the token and says it cannot be undone. A box for a code goes to the Connect a device page. The tokens section leaves the profile, and the account menu's "Access tokens and devices" opens `/tokens/`.
- **Connect a device:** before anything else it says to connect only a code the person started themselves, then what is asking, the code and when it ends, a name (refused in words when empty or over 80 characters, before anything is sent) and when it stops (Never first), and Connect or Deny. Signed out, it says to log in and that the code in the address is kept while they do. Connected, it shows the `device-connected` mascot under an h2 (the page keeps its own h1) and says what happens next; Denied says nothing was connected. The page still asks for no referrer.
- **Home (AH1, AH6):** two columns, "Needs you" and "Where you play" on the left and "What you run" (the campaigns a person runs and the repositories they work on) on the right, then "Continue" and "What do you want to do?". A campaign appears under "Where you play" when the person holds a seat in it and under "What you run" for any other role. "Needs you" holds tasks, never news: for now a repository still a draft, with the list growing as more is computed (updates waiting, a link about to end, a new player); with none it says the person is all caught up and that notifications are in the bell, so the unread list leaves Home. "Continue" is the last three pages opened here, kept in this browser only (never a page whose address is a secret: Home, Connect a device, Join). The first-run choice for a new account is unchanged for now.
- **First run (ADR 0416, D27):** a new account sees the mascot beside the welcome and two doors: "I run a game" (Set up a library, only for an account that may create one) and "I'm joining a game" (a field to paste an invite link or its code, and Join). "Just looking around? Browse public repositories" appears only where public repositories are on. The image is `first-run-welcome` from the brand's mascots, falling back to the earlier welcome image and then to a placeholder with the words until the illustrators' is delivered. The words "library" and "game" are the design's; the nav and the setup page still say "table" until they are renamed together.
- **Notifications:** a 760px page with the count ("3 unread", or "Nothing unread"), an All and Unread choice and "Mark all as read" over a quiet list; an unread one is marked by a dot and the word "Unread." for a screen reader, shows when it came in relative to now (the exact time on hover and under the text when opened), and has "Mark read". Opening one still shows its text and where it came from. Nothing to read shows the `caught-up` mascot under "You're all caught up". Notifications you have sent, with who has read them, stay below, shown on request. The bell, the 30-second poll and the page's own refresh are unchanged.
- **Libraries (AH7):** with two or more libraries, `/tenants/` opens on an index of cards (name, the person's role, a line of description, counts of campaigns and repositories) and "Set up a library" sits in the page heading for those who may create one; inside a library a "Libraries / name" breadcrumb leads back. With one library the page opens straight into it, as before. Campaigns inside libraries and Repositories as one area follow separately.
- **Campaigns in their library (AH3, AH8):** a campaign's card in its library now holds the person's own seat when they have one (their characters there, using one from another campaign, stopping use of a linked one, leaving), above the tabs the people who manage it already had. The cross-library campaigns page is gone: `/campaigns/`, `/overview/` and `/characters/` forward to the libraries, the "Campaigns" tab leaves the navigation, and Home's "What you run" links to the libraries.
- **"Table" becomes "library" in the hub's own words:** the navigation item and the setup page say "Set up a library" and "Your library is ready", and Home offers "Join with an invite link" (a link can lead to a library, a campaign or a role). What a library's campaign calls its game master (ADR 0294) still says "table", because that is a table of players, not the thing being set up.
- **Repositories, My repositories and Discover (the frame):** the three pages take the same heading as the other pages (a title and a line under it), and an empty Studio or Shelf says so in a page state with the mascot. Nothing else about them changes in this step: the one Repositories area (AH2) and its role-driven tabs follow as their own slices.
- **One Repositories entry (AH2, first step):** the navigation has one "Repositories" item, shown to someone who runs a library or works on a repository (or may make one). Its page opens with "Your repositories" (Studio's list as cards that open Studio, with "New repository") above "Used by your libraries" (Shelf as before); a repository's page, a copy and the update views show only their own part. "My repositories" leaves the navigation, and Studio's sidebar links back to the page. The tabs of a repository still live in Studio and Shelf; merging them under one address is the next step.
- **One address for a repository (AH2, second step):** a repository one works on opens at `/repositories/?repository=…` (and the form for a new one at `/repositories/?new=1`), with the same workspace and tabs as before; an address that names a library (`?tenant=…`) or none opens what libraries use, as before. The page keeps only the half the address asks for, and a step back to the other half loads the page again. `/studio/` stays as a page that forwards, with its query, to the new address, so bookmarks and sent links keep working. The tab set already follows the role (People and Releases appear for those who may use them); a "Use in a library" tab is not added, because a library's page for a repository is where it is copied and updated.
- **Discover (AH5):** the list is a grid of cards (a letter or the cover, the name, the summary, the tags, who published it and the release), the search sits above its four filters, and a repository's page has two columns: what is inside on one side and "Copy it into a library" with the facts on the other (the copy first on a phone). It stays a tab of its own, shown only where public repositories are on. The profile preview in Studio draws the same card.
- **What the campaigns page said, kept in the library (audit):** an administrator who opted out of a campaign still sees its card, with "Admin" as their role and "This campaign's people aren't shown to you." in place of the roster (the library no longer fails to open for them); a person's own row in a people list says "(you)".
- **Beings leaves the hub (AH3):** the "Beings" navigation item, the page's list and the hand-off panel are removed; `/beings/` forwards, with its query, to Lorenzo Beings where the registry says it is (or says it has its own app). Listing and searching, making a being, renaming and handing one to a player are in Beings itself. What the hub's page also showed, a repository's beings for reading, is a repository's entries in its own page.
- **Dialogs, not the browser's confirm (D26):** what cannot be undone asks first in the shell's dialog, which names what is lost and has a button that says what it does (`lib/askFirst.ts`): leaving a library or a campaign, stepping down as GM, removing a player, stopping the use of a character, revoking an invite link, deleting the account, stopping an invitation, resetting or removing the subscriber copy, removing a cover, and applying a breaking or hidden update. Where the shell is not there the browser's own confirm asks. The prompt that guards unsaved changes when a tab or a repository is left stays the browser's own, because it must answer before the page moves.
- **Joining (D27):** the page is a reading page with the same heading as the others; its four moments (the preview signed out, the preview signed in, done, and the one message for every link that does not work) are as before, and the dead link and the done moment carry a small mascot picture, decoration only. The words, the referrer rule and the link that survives the login are unchanged.
- **A library by tab (AH7, AH8):** a library has four tabs, Overview, Campaigns, People and Settings, each there only while it has something for the person. Overview (administrators) shows how many people, campaigns and repositories it has, the repositories it draws on with their state beside a link to all of them, and what happened lately. Campaigns holds the campaign cards and the form that makes one. People holds the roster, the invite links and a word to everyone. Settings holds the name and description (Edit), the picture, "Leave this library" and the deletion that asks for the name. A player or GM who is not a member sees the campaigns only.
- **A library's Settings as a form with the save bar:** General (name, link name, description) is a set of fields an administrator always has in Settings, not an Edit button; changing any of them opens the shell's save bar ("2 changes not saved"), which saves them together, discards them, and says "Saved" in a toast. A change made to the library elsewhere meanwhile is refused in words, naming the library, and keeps what was typed. A repository keeps its Edit button.
- **A repository for one who uses it (AH2, AH9):** a library's page for a repository has two tabs, Overview (what it is: the description, what is inside, a look inside, its entries and stat groups) and "Use in a library" (what a library does with it: copy it, check it for updates, what it is built on, and the library's history with it). It opens on "Use in a library" while there is something to do, else on Overview. For one who works on the repository, Studio's tabs (Overview, Profile, People, Releases, Libraries using it, Built on, Activity) follow the role as before.
- **Needs you holds all its tasks (AH6):** Home computes four kinds of task from what the person may read, never news: an update announced for a repository one of their libraries copied (from the library's repository list, `shelfState`), an invite link that still works and ends within three days (a library's links for its Owner, a campaign's for whoever runs it), a new player who joined a campaign they run in the last three days (from the library's activity log, as a count for each campaign), and a repository of theirs that is still a draft. A read that is refused or fails gives no task and no error. The lists are in the order the design draws them.
- **A library that is not theirs is a page that says so (screen 12):** an address that names a library the person has no part in is the shell's "This page isn't available" state with a way back to Home, and the address stays as asked; it no longer opens another of their libraries in its place.
- **Leaving an unsaved profile asks in the dialog too:** the last `window.confirm` of the hub is the shell's dialog. A tab's `canLeave` may now answer later, once the person has, so `select` can return a promise.
- **The hub in the shell as drawn (a check of the built pages against the design):** the header's places are Home, Libraries, Repositories and Discover, as the design has them, with the app's icon beside its name; Profile, Notifications, Access tokens and devices are behind the account button, which shows the name over the address and the unread count, and "Set up a library" is a button beside "What you run" and on Libraries. The status bar says "Synced" with the time and "N need you" on Home.
- **Every page is in the shell's own container for its template (D21):** Home, Libraries, Repositories and Discover are `list-detail` (or `home`, 1120), Set up a library, Profile and Access tokens are forms (1040), Notifications, Connect a device and Join are reading (680). Libraries and Studio had broken out of the page with the old `full-width` (100vw) and the pages without a template had the old `wrap`; both are gone, so no page is 100% wide or has an inner wrapper of its own.
- **Home is the Home of the design:** "Home" with who it is and how many things need them; on the left "Needs you" (a row each: icon, what, where, and the button) and "Where you play" (a row each, with Leave, which asks first, and "Join with an invite link"), on the right "What you run" (a row for each library, with what it holds, the person's part and "Updates waiting", and for each repository, with Draft or Published; and "Set up a library" and "Create a repository"); then "Continue" (a card each, with the app and when it was opened) and "What do you want to do?" (every deployed app, as a card with its icon and what it is for). The recent changes to what a person's characters hold stay, under "Where you play".
