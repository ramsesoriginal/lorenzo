# 0441 - Sessions moves into the web shell

Status: accepted, decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-08. Part of [RFC 0050](../rfcs/0050-the-web-interface.md); it implements the Sessions part of UI-E ([#1016](https://github.com/ramsesoriginal/lorenzo/issues/1016)) with [ADR 0420](0420-calendars-and-sessions-redesign.md) and the design in [docs/design/web/calendars-sessions](../design/web/calendars-sessions/README.md) (CS1, CS4 to CS6, screens 14 to 24, 26, 31 and 32), after [Calendars](0440-calendars-moves-into-the-web-shell.md), on the shell of [ADR 0426](0426-the-web-shell-grows-in-app-shell-opt-in.md).

## Context

Sessions draws its own page as a one-row header, a `wrap` and a footer, with the tabs My Sessions and Scenes. The design puts it in the web shell's frame, with Sessions, Scenes and Lived order as tabs (CS5), a Home of the next Session and Sessions by campaign, and a Session page of nine regions with a View menu (CS4, CS6).

## Decision

Slices, each its own pull request on the one before; this record grows a bullet for each.

- **The frame is the web shell** (`AppPage shell="web"`, the `list-detail` template). The tabs are Sessions (current on a Session's page too), Scenes (current on a Scene's) and Lived order, the last two for a library. The palette, the apps sheet and the status bar are the shell's, and the account menu holds Appearance, so the old light/dark toggle is gone; without an account the palette's Change theme opens Appearance. The e2e tests that drove the old header (the logo, the toggle) drive the shell instead.
- **Home as drawn (screen 14):** "Sessions" with a primary **New Session** that opens the form that makes one (for whoever runs a campaign; nobody else is offered it, and the form is not on the page until then); **Next**, the soonest Session whose real-world date has not gone by (an undated Session is never next, and no in-world "now" is read from anywhere); then each campaign's Sessions together, in date order, a row with its date, a chip for **Past** or **Next**, and the person's part, **You are a GM** or **You play**. The logic (`nextSession`, `stateOf`, `byCampaign`) is a pure module with tests. Not drawn: the design's "starts Greening 3" and "Not placed in order", which need a read of each Session's start period and of the campaign's order for every row.
- **The Session page is one page of regions with a View menu (CS4, CS6, screens 15 to 18):** the nine parts are Details, Roster, Notes, References, Game time, Scenes, Played, Campaign order and Lived order. A **View** button over the page lists the views (**Prepare**: details, roster, notes and Scenes; **Played**: the played record and notes; **All**) and each part, ticked where it is shown, and **Save this view…**, which names the parts shown now. A Session **opens in Prepare every time** (CS7); a person's own views are kept in this browser (`lorenzo:sessions:views`, named in the privacy policy) and are theirs to pick. A line at the foot says what is hidden, and that hiding a part hides only the drawing: nothing is deleted and nothing about who may see it changes. A region drawn again after a save keeps what the view says of it. The logic (`PARTS`, `viewName`, `footLine`, `withView`) is a pure module with tests. A part a person may not use is not there to show, whatever the view says.
- **The status bar says the Session's in-world date (CS1):** on a Session's page it reads "You are a GM · In-world: Greening 22, Year 4" (the part the person has, and the Session's start period, as the Session says it), and nothing on any other page, since a campaign has no "now". It follows an edit of the start.
- **The parts are cards in two columns from 1100px** (one below it), in the order the View menu lists them, each with a control at the end of its heading to hide it (the same choice as unticking it in the menu). A part that folds has no such control in its summary, since a control inside a `<summary>` is nested interactive content (the accessibility check found it); the menu reaches every part.
- **A Scene's page is cards in two columns too (screen 21):** its details, references and notes, in the same grid as a Session's parts. **No Session yet (screen 24)** has a heading and what to expect (a GM assigns you; an invite adds you to a campaign), and no New Session for someone who runs no campaign. The design's illustration (`empty-sessions`) is not drawn: the mascot art is not in the brand yet.

## Consequences

- The pages' contents move to the design in the slices that follow.
