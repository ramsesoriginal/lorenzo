Scrolling

Two small helpers for a flex .row that holds a growing panel, and a pair that lets a long list scroll inside the page instead of making it longer.

Grow

.grow takes whatever space is left in a .row, so a label and its actions stay at the ends and the middle stretches.

Sticky top

A .row centers its items, so when one of them grows taller - an edit panel opening beside the title - the others drift down to the middle. .row.sticky-top keeps them at the top. Without it:

Vertical window and mask

.vertical-window caps a list at 70% of the viewport height and scrolls it. .vertical-mask fades the top edge once something has scrolled out above it, and the bottom edge while more is still below. Browsers without scroll-driven animations show the window, unfaded.
